What's new and how to check it

  1. Icons: duplicate, reset, delete, undo and the rest are matching line icons. Hover one to see its name.
  2. Summary first: total debt, monthly payments, debt-free date and interest left sit above the list.
  3. Numbers line up on the right so you can compare down a column.
  4. Payoff bars: each debt has a small plum bar; the longest payoff is a full bar.
  5. Tags with symbols: ≈ Roughly, ? Guess, ⚠ Promo, so they read without color.
  6. Area stripe: the Debt title has a plum stripe; sub-tabs show colored dots and progress (3/4).
  7. Empty boxes stand out: filled boxes have a softer border; the car loan's empty APR has a strong border and says "Add APR".
  8. Saved moment: change any number and press Tab; the row flashes green and "✓ Saved" appears.
  9. Sticky: scroll the page and the top bar stays; scroll the list and the column names and totals stay.
  10. Show the math: click "$119" or "21 months".
  11. Change since last session: the ▲ ▼ lines under the summary numbers.
  12. Keyboard: press / to jump to Find; press Enter in a row to add a new one.
  13. Density: Compact vs Comfortable at the top of this page.
  14. Client view: the eye icon in the top bar makes text bigger for screen sharing.
  15. Groups: Credit cards and Loans with subtotals; click a group name to fold it.
  16. Totals row pinned at the bottom of the list.
  17. Print: the one-pager prints without buttons or dark backgrounds (built into the app, not shown here).
  18. Pick a font and tell me which, plus anything that still feels off.
