What the game's glossary says today, word for word:

- Big E: "A big emergency: losing a job, a medical emergency, replacing the car. Well over $1,000, and it can run for months. Your full cushion, the months of spending you set aside, is what covers a Big E."
- Little e: "A small emergency, under about $1,000: a flat tire, a vet visit, a broken phone. Your first $1,000 is there so a little e never has to go on a card."
- Oxygen mask first: "On a plane you put on your own mask before you help anyone else. With money it means: cover your basic expenses, clear high-interest debt, get retirement on track, and give a home or tuition goal a date. After that, money can go to other people."

- Freedom number: "25 times a year of your spending. At that point your investments can pay for your life."
