# Good morning: what to try first on your phone

Open https://sapphirestoneage.github.io/Slam/game/ and pull down to refresh one time.

1. **Menu, then "Add another person".** Do setup as a new person (about 17 taps). At the end you get the new reveal:
   strengths and weak spots as numbers, your element, "Does this feel accurate?", "Which do you want to improve most?".
2. **Next Steps.** Today / This week is now at the top. The board under it uses plain words. Turn on the gamer switch
   under Me and look again.
3. **+ Log, "I paid a debt".** Five taps. "Paid from" opens by itself now and its first answer pulses.
4. **Safe to spend?, $200.** Two taps. No red.
5. **My Goals, "Break in case of emergency".** Tap through the five tabs. With the gamer switch on, the tab "Plan"
   shows Economic / Money and the three others.
6. **Menu, "Product X-ray".** Try "Insurance that also saves" with a client's monthly payment.
7. **Name button, then "Kids mode".** Add "My teen" and do lesson 1.
8. **Menu, "Fire drills".** Open one. Look for "How long it lasts" and "A small real dose".
9. **Menu, "My plan as a spreadsheet", then "Bring an edited sheet back".** Easier on a laptop.
10. **Menu, "Your gear".** Mark a slot "Don't have it" and see the four new answers.
11. On a laptop: https://sapphirestoneage.github.io/Slam/aliyah.html and https://sapphirestoneage.github.io/Slam/cards.html

Then read, in this order: `QUESTIONS.md` number 573 (question (a) is about the Tier 7 zip file in the chat),
`STATUS.md`, `USABILITY.md`, `DECISIONS.md`.

To undo any of it: each item is one commit in the Git history.
