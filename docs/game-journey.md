# SLAM the Game: the journey, era by era

Written from the code on Nov 21 2026 (game/engine.js ERAS, ERA_NODES and NODES, plus game/eras.js). When the steps change, this file should change with them.

**How an era opens.** The gate is worked out from the player's numbers. From Future You on, the era also needs its starred key moment. Steps can be done early. A key moment, once reached, stays reached. 85% of an era's steps is the mark for calling it solid.

**Every step** can be marked done, working on it, already done, or doesn't apply to me. Steps marked "each year" start fresh every January.

## 0. Digging Out

- Gate: Where everyone below zero starts
- Steps: none. It is where everyone below zero starts.

## 1. Buffer

- Gate: Set aside $1,000
- Steps:
  - List every debt with its rate
  - Put the minimums on autopay
  - Keep a small buffer in checking
  - Set aside $1,000

## 2. Free Money

- Gate: Get the full match from your job (or have no match to get)
- Steps:
  - Read your pay stub
  - Know where your 401(k) is

## 3. Debt Free

- Gate: No debt charging 8% or more
- Steps:
  - Pick a payoff method
  - No new card charges
  - Call and ask for a lower rate
  - Pay off your first debt
  - All 8%+ debt gone

## 4. Fortress

- Gate: Save your age divided by 5, in months of spending
- Steps:
  - Open a separate high-yield savings account
  - Know every deductible
  - Renters or home insurance
  - Build your full emergency fund
  - Pay statements in full

## 5. Future You

- Gate: Fill your Roth IRA, HSA and workplace plan (now real steps: the key moment below)
- Steps:
  - Check your disability coverage
  - Term life, if anyone depends on you
  - Name beneficiaries on every account
  - Credit score 740 or higher
  - Open a Roth IRA
  - Fill your Roth IRA this year (each year, ranks 25% / 50% / 75% / 100%)
  - Open an HSA (only when it applies)
  - Fill your HSA this year (each year, ranks 25% / 50% / 75% / 100%) (only when it applies)
  - Set your workplace plan to your target (only when it applies)
  - ★ Future you gets paid first (ticks itself)

## 6. Quiet Quitting FI

- Gate: Only a Roth IRA each year would still get you there (from the numbers), plus the key moment
- Steps:
  - See your bare-minimum plan
  - Check Social Security for each person. From: Mike Piper, Oblivious Investor
  - Set your retirement spending
  - ★ The reevaluation moment

## 7. Coast

- Gate: Invested enough to retire on time with nothing more added (from the numbers), plus the key moment
- Steps:
  - See your bridge years
  - Map your early access. From: IRS rules, the Mad Fientist's Roth ladder
  - Check the middle-class trap meter. From: Scott Trench and Mindy Jensen, BiggerPockets Money
  - Pick your shape from here
  - ★ The second reevaluation

## 8. Freedom

- Gate: Investments cover your spending (from the numbers), plus the key moment
- Steps:
  - Pick how you will take money out. From: Bengen, Guyton and Klinger, the Bogleheads, Christine Benz
  - Set up bracket filling and your health insurance target. From: Go Curry Cracker, Root of Good
  - Plan your first year's paycheck
  - The "won the game" risk check. From: William Bernstein
  - ★ Your first year of the drawdown plan is written (ticks itself)

## 9. Legacy

- Gate: Freedom reached, and people or causes to build for (from the numbers), plus the key moment
- Steps:
  - Review who inherits each account
  - Make a giving plan
  - Estate basics: a will, and who decides (ranks 1 of 3 / 2 of 3 / All 3)
  - Make a family money plan. From: Money dates, made popular by Tori Dunlap and others
  - ★ Your graduation choice

## Which account first

One line worked out from the retirement chain in What depends on what (budget, retirement spending, account mix, taxes, your number). It shows on Home while Free Money or Future You is next, on the Roth, workplace plan and key moment steps of Future You, on the payday split, and in the menu. It is marked Guess, Close or Exact by how many of its five parts are real, and its Why replays the chain. If a missing part could change the answer, that part is asked for next.

## Gaps this closes

- Future You's gate was one yes or no answer. It is now steps a player can do, with a key moment.
- Eras 6 to 9 had a computed status and nothing to do. Each now has steps and a key moment.
- The retirement chain did not reach back into the early eras. The account line now does.
