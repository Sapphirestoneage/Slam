# First-timer walks, Nov 15 2026 (Run 3)

Eight pages on a phone-width screen (390 wide) in Chrome: the front door, What is my first move, Start at the finish
line, How do I handle money, When am I debt free, How long would my money last, Where am I on the skill tree, and Is
this card right for me. Two walks each:

1. **Follow the pulse.** A first-timer who only taps what the page points at. Does that alone reach an answer?
2. **Type like a person.** Tap a box, type, move to the next box, never tap outside. Does the answer show with the
   keyboard still up?

Also checked on every page: sideways scroll, tap targets under 40px, stray words (null, undefined, NaN), script errors.
